2. What Data We Collect

Static Website

This website is a static HTML site. We do not operate a server that stores personal data. No cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics tools are used directly on these pages.

This website is hosted on Vercel Inc. When you visit the site, Vercel may automatically log standard server access data including your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and timestamp. This is standard server logging and is handled by Vercel. For details, see Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Purchases via Whop.com

When you purchase a product, you are redirected to Whop.com. Any data you provide during checkout (name, email, payment details) is collected and processed by Whop, not by PFH Studios. For details, see Whop's Privacy Policy.

We may receive your email address from Whop after a purchase to fulfil your order and send product updates. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.
